# Artwork prompts

Generated with the Codex CLI's image generation tool (`codex exec`, codex-cli 0.153.4) on 2026-09-14, following the
art direction in [`brand/BRAND.md`](../../brand/BRAND.md#imagery). The PNGs here are the originals; the site uses JPEG
copies in `website/src/media/`.

Shared direction, included in every prompt:

> Art direction (from the Second Shift brand guide): painterly industrial illustration of a factory floor at night: conveyor belts, assembling machines and pipes under warm work lights in muted steel and rust tones, with one cool cyan light source from a helmet visor or a screen. Low saturation, heavy shadow, no text, no logos, no UI, no people's faces, not the Factorio art style (no pixel-art sprites, no game screenshot look). Palette anchors: night steel #1c1a18, plate #25221f, paper #ebe5d9, rust and amber work light, one accent of visor cyan #7cc4d8.
